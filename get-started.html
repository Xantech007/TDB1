What you need to get started

Quick checklist for online registration

To register for EasyWeb Online Banking or apply for products online smoothly, please ensure you have the following ready:

  • Your TD Account or Access Card Number (or active TD Credit Card).
  • A valid Canadian phone number capable of receiving SMS verification codes for Two-Step Verification.
  • Personal identification details (such as your Social Insurance Number [SIN] for tax reporting on interest-bearing accounts).
  • An active email address to receive statements and banking confirmations.
