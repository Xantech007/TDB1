Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your TD Savings Account

What is a savings account?

A savings account is a deposit account designed for saving cash. It helps you achieve short, medium, and long-term goals like a vacation, school expenses, or an emergency fund while earning interest over time.

What types of Savings Accounts do you offer?

We offer flexible options including the TD ePremium Savings Account, TD Every Day Savings Account, and TD Growth Savings Account. Current customers can apply directly online through EasyWeb or the TD App.

What is the difference between a chequing and savings account?

A chequing account is used for day-to-day transactions like buying groceries or paying bills. A savings account is dedicated to saving cash for future goals and earns higher interest.

How do I open a TD savings account?

You can quickly open an account online if you are the age of majority in your province, or in any TD Canada Trust branch using a valid passport or government-issued photo ID.

How can I automate my savings?

Grow your money by enrolling in automated programs like Simply Save (saving spare change on debit purchases) or the Pre-Authorized Transfer Service.

How is interest calculated on my bank account?

Interest is calculated daily based on your account's daily closing balance and paid on the last business day of the month.
