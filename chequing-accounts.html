What is a Chequing Account?

A chequing account is the most basic transactional account you can have. It’s considered an account you can use every day.

Some of the things you can do with your chequing account includes: Deposits (cash and cheques), withdraw money (at ATMs, bank tellers, etc.), one-time purchases (groceries, gas, etc.), one-time or pre-authorized payments, transfers (between accounts of the same institution), and Interac e-Transfer® transactions.

Your funds in this account can be easily accessed at an ATM, online, by phone, or in a branch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Chequing accounts

How can I close a TD Chequing Account?

To close your TD chequing account, simply visit the TD branch closest to you or call TD Easyline at 1-888-663-3279. Certain conditions may apply.

What is the difference between a chequing and savings account?

Chequing accounts are usually used for day-to-day banking needs while savings accounts can help you achieve your savings goals.

Are cheques free?

Depending on the chequing account type you open, your new chequing account may include free cheques. To view which accounts waive specified cheque fees, please visit the TD Public Site – Personal Bank Accounts page to view account features.

Does TD offer any monthly no fee bank accounts?

Yes, the Minimum Chequing Account has no monthly fee for recipients of the Guaranteed Income Supplement or beneficiaries of a Registered Disability Savings Plan or Indigenous Peoples from Canada or Newcomers to Canada (for 1st year in Canada). The TD Student Chequing Account has no monthly fee with unlimited transactions per month for Students and Youth.

Can I earn interest by keeping money in my chequing account?

Yes, you can earn interest on the TD Student Chequing Account and U.S. Daily Interest Chequing Account.

Do all chequing accounts come with a debit card?

Yes. All TD chequing accounts come with a debit card. You can also add your debit card to your digital wallet. Certain chequing accounts also allow you to make unlimited transactions with your debit card.

How can I keep my monthly fees low?

Understanding how often you’ll use your chequing account and for what will help determine the type of account and services you need. Some of our chequing accounts will waive the monthly fee if you keep the minimum balance at the end of each day of the month.

How can I open a chequing account?

Most of our accounts can be opened online in less than 5 minutes. Make sure you have a valid email address, employer information, and valid ID (either a valid passport or Canadian driver’s license) to verify your ID.
