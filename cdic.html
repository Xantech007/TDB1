Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC)

Protecting Your Deposits – Learn how TD Bank Group members safeguard your hard-earned money.

What is the CDIC?

TD Bank and its Canadian deposit-issuing subsidiaries are proud members of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC).

CDIC is a federal crown corporation – a part of the government of Canada – created by Parliament in 1967 to protect money on deposit in the event a member institution becomes insolvent. CDIC protects eligible deposits to a maximum of \$100,000 per depositor and per insured category.

Coverage is free and automatic; customers do not have to apply for it. If a member institution failed, CDIC would reimburse insured deposits (including interest) up to \$100,000 per insured category.

Eligibility for Deposit Insurance

To be eligible for deposit insurance, a deposit must be:

  • payable in Canada
  • in Canadian, US, or foreign currency

Eligible Deposits Include:

  • Chequing & Savings Accounts
  • Term deposits / Guaranteed Investment Certificates (GICs)
  • Money orders, Certified Cheques & Bank Drafts issued by a CDIC member institution
  • Certain In-transit Payments from a CDIC member institution such as payroll and Interac e-Transfers

TD Bank Group CDIC Member Institutions:

The following entities are CDIC member institutions (operating under the trade name TD Canada Trust):

  • The Toronto-Dominion Bank
  • TD Mortgage Corporation
  • TD Pacific Mortgage Corporation
  • The Canada Trust Company
